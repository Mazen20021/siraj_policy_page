1 Overview

Siraj (سراج) is a prayer-time application for Android that calculates accurate Islamic prayer times, plays the azan (call to prayer) and iqama, shows the qibla direction, tracks the Hijri calendar, and provides a home-screen widget with prayer times and countdowns.

This policy explains what information the app uses, why it is used, and how it is handled. The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers — everything is processed on your device.

2 Information the App Uses

  • Location (GPS / network): used only on your device to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction. Your coordinates are never uploaded or shared. If you prefer, you can instead set a manual location in Settings, in which case the location permission is not required at all.
  • Preferences you choose: your settings — calculation method, language, theme, azan sound selection, notification choices, and the last used location — are stored locally on your device only.
  • No account, no personal data: the app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, or phone number, and does not collect any usage statistics.

3 App Permissions & Why They Are Used

  • Location (fine / coarse): needed to compute your accurate prayer times and qibla. Can be replaced by a manually chosen location.
  • Notifications: shows the azan / iqama alerts at prayer time, even when the app is closed. You can turn notifications off at any time.
  • Exact alarms: schedules the daily prayer alerts so they fire exactly at the calculated time.
  • Full-screen intent: shows the azan alert screen at prayer time while the device is locked or the app is in the background.
  • Internet: used only to check whether a newer version of the app exists on Google Play (when you tap “Check for Update”). No data is sent.
  • Boot completed: re-schedules your daily prayer alerts after the device restarts.
  • Vibrate: adds a gentle vibration to the prayer alerts.
  • Home-screen widget: displays prayer times and a live countdown on your home screen; all data shown comes from your device.
No ads, no tracking, no analytics. The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party trackers of any kind.

4 How the Information Is Used

All information is used exclusively for the features you see in the app:

  • Calculating prayer times for the five daily prayers and the Hijri date.
  • Determining the qibla direction for your location.
  • Playing the azan and iqama audio at the correct time.
  • Showing prayer reminders and the home-screen widget.

Your location and settings are processed entirely on your device and are never transmitted to us or to any third party.

5 Third-Party Services

The only third-party service involved is Google Play, which distributes the app and, if you choose to check for updates, may handle that request through Google's own services. Google's privacy practices are governed by Google's privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any information with advertisers, analytics companies, or any other third parties — because we hold no personal data in the first place.

6 Data Storage & Security

All app data — settings, preferences, and the last used location — is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. No data leaves your device, so there is nothing to secure on any server.

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device completely.

7 Children's Privacy

Siraj is safe and appropriate for users of all ages, including children. Because the app does not collect any personal information, there is no personal data of children (or anyone else) to collect or process.
